Bet: each Kalshi line can be bought two ways: yes at the ask, or no at 1 − the bid (the QB stays under, or against a score market). "Positive return after fees" lists only bets with a positive Return. "Best per market" keeps each QB's or market's largest gap on each side, because its lines move together.

Gap = the model's chance of that side winning minus its price; the range under it and the dots on the bar are the gap with the QBs' form at the low and high end of its likely range. clear means the whole range is above the price. Return = expected profit per $1 after Kalshi's taker fee (7% × price × (1 − price), rounded up to the cent, per contract). It assumes the model's chance is right; these gaps have not been graded against results yet, so treat them as questions, not bets.

Left out: lines already decided, games that are over, and prices of 1¢ or 99¢ and up. Score markets cover the game, each half and quarter; quarters don't count overtime, the 2nd half and the game do; a tied game pays half on either team. For information only.
