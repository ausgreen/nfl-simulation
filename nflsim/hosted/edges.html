Each Kalshi line priced both ways: buying yes at the ask, or no at 1 − the bid (the price to bet the QB stays under, or against a score market). Gap = the model's chance of that side winning minus its price. Return = expected profit per $1, after Kalshi's taker fee (7% × price × (1 − price), rounded up to the cent, per contract). Lines already decided (the QB is past them) and games that are over are left out; so are prices of 1¢ or 99¢ and up. Dots on the bar and the range under Gap: the likely range of the chance (passing yards only). Score markets: the game, each half and quarter (quarters don't count overtime; the 2nd half and the game do); a tied game pays half on either team. For information only.
