About these numbers

Updated from ESPN's live feed, which runs a few seconds behind TV and can briefly lag a score or a change of possession. The numbers follow the feed as it is.

Win chance and passing-yard chances come from simulating the rest of the game 4,000 times from the current situation; the drive outcomes from 10,000 simulations of the current drive; the next-snap states from 20,000 one-play simulations.

Game simulator (nflsim/sim/): play-by-play tables from the five seasons before this one, team offense and defense ratings, each game tuned to v3's expected yards and the lines' implied points. Next-snap states: 20,000 one-play simulations; drive outcomes: 10,000 simulations of the current drive; QB outlook and win chance: 4,000 simulations of the rest of the game. Known weaknesses: overconfident late in games (timeouts, onside kicks, penalties are not modeled); overtime is not simulated. For information only.
